Leave blank to index all mail. When a date is set, messages received before that date are excluded from the index. Works alongside account and folder filters. Changing this applies incrementally (no full rebuild needed).

Off by default for privacy. Encrypted emails are indexed by subject/sender only, so their decrypted text is never written to the on-disk index. Turn this on to make their bodies searchable — their decrypted content will then be stored unencrypted in the index.

How much of each email's body is indexed (after quoted replies are removed). Smaller keeps the index lighter, so it loads faster and uses less memory — important on machines with limited RAM. Larger makes more of very long emails searchable. Changing this needs a rebuild.

Index

Rebuild re-indexes all of your mail from scratch. Verify & repair checks the index against your folders and fixes any drift — removing stale entries and indexing anything missing. Refresh status re-counts how many messages are eligible to be indexed (folder metadata only — safe to use at any time, including during a build). Clear index deletes the search index from disk — including the decrypted contents of any encrypted emails you opted to index. Search stays empty until you rebuild.
